About inn Partner

inn Partner is a business-management companion app for businesses (such as shops and gyms) that operate on the inn platform. It is used by business owners and staff to manage their business profile, catalog, and incoming customer orders.

inn Partner accounts are created and provisioned by the platform operator for approved business partners. The app is not self-registrable by the general public and is not intended for consumer/shopper use — for that, see the separate inn customer app.

Information We Collect

We collect and process the following categories of information:

  • Account and identity information. The email address and display name associated with your partner account, and your Firebase account identifier (UID). Sign-in to inn Partner uses email and password.
  • Partner account information. Your account status and the identifier of the business (shop or gym) linked to your account.
  • Business profile information you provide. Business name and description, phone and WhatsApp numbers, address, business location coordinates, opening hours, delivery areas, and business category. Business address and map coordinates are entered manually by you to describe your storefront's location to customers — inn Partner does not access or track your device's live GPS location.
  • Uploaded media. Photos you choose from your device's photo gallery — for example a business logo, cover photo, gallery photos, or product/menu photos. Uploads use gallery selection only; inn Partner does not access your device camera directly.
  • Order and customer fulfillment information. To help you fulfill orders placed with your business, the app shows order details including the customer's name and phone number, delivery area or address (or delivery coordinates provided at checkout), ordered items and quantities, prices, the selected payment method label, order status, and any notes associated with accepting, rejecting, or cancelling an order.
  • Push notification data. A notification/device token, a device identifier generated by the app, platform, and language preference, stored against your partner account so we can deliver order alerts to your device.
  • Technical data processed by Firebase. Standard infrastructure-level data (such as request metadata) that Google Firebase processes automatically to operate authentication, database, storage, and messaging. inn Partner does not integrate a separate analytics or crash-reporting SDK, and contains no advertising.

How We Use Information

  • To authenticate you and identify your account and business.
  • To let you manage your business profile, catalog, and media.
  • To show you orders placed with your business and let you accept, reject, track, and complete them.
  • To send push notifications about new orders and order updates.
  • To operate, maintain, and secure the service, prevent misuse, and communicate necessary service updates.

We do not use your information for advertising, and inn Partner does not contain advertising.

Firebase and Service Providers

inn Partner is built on Google Firebase, which we use as a service provider (data processor) for:

  • Firebase Authentication — account sign-in.
  • Cloud Firestore — storing account, business, and order data.
  • Firebase Storage — storing uploaded images.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging — delivering push notifications.
  • Cloud Functions — running backend business logic securely.

Firebase/Google processes this data on our behalf to provide these functions, subject to Google's own security and data-processing terms. Using a service provider is not the same as selling your data — see Data Sharing below.

Push Notifications

inn Partner uses push notifications, primarily to alert you to new orders and order status changes in near real time. Notifications can be turned on or off at any time from the app's Settings screen. If notifications are disabled at the device or app level, the associated notification token is no longer used to deliver alerts.

Business and Order Information

Orders are placed by customers of the inn platform. As a business partner, inn Partner shows you the limited customer and order information necessary to prepare and fulfill an order placed with your business — such as the customer's name, phone number, and delivery details. This information is provided to you strictly to operate your business on the platform, and should not be used for any unrelated purpose.

Media and Uploaded Content

Images you upload (logos, cover photos, gallery photos, product or menu photos) are stored using Firebase Storage and associated with your business profile or catalog. These images may be displayed to customers browsing or ordering from your business on the inn platform.

Data Sharing

inn Partner does not sell personal data.

Information is shared only as follows:

  • With Google Firebase, acting as our service provider, as described above.
  • Within the inn platform, to the extent necessary to operate the marketplace — for example, your business profile and catalog are shown to customers, and your account is visible to platform administrators who operate and support the service.
  • If required by law, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of inn, our users, or others.

We do not share your information with third parties for their own independent marketing purposes.

Data Retention

We retain account, business, and order data for as long as your partner account is active, and afterward for as long as reasonably necessary for legitimate business purposes such as recordkeeping, dispute resolution, and legal compliance. Uploaded media are retained while linked to your business profile or catalog, or to an order, and may be removed or replaced when you update your profile or catalog.

Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures — including Firebase Authentication, Firestore and Storage security rules, and encrypted transport — to help protect the information we process. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Account Access, Correction, and Deletion

inn Partner accounts are created and managed by the platform operator/administrator. The app does not currently offer in-app self-registration or a self-service option to delete your account or associated data.

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your partner account or associated data, please contact us using the details below.

Children's Privacy

inn Partner is a business-management tool intended for business owners and staff operating a partner business on the inn platform. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children through this app.
